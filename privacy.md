# Privacy Statement — Finance Cockpit

Finance Cockpit is a personal, non-commercial tool. It reads only its owner's own bank
account data via Enable Banking (AIS / PSD2) and stores that data in the owner's own
private Notion workspace.

- The data is not shared with any third parties.
- The data is not used for any commercial purpose.
- The bank connection is provided by the licensed service Enable Banking Oy, regulated by
  the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA).
- Consent for account access can be withdrawn at any time via your bank or the service.
